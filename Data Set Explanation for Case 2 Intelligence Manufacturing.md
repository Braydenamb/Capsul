Equipment Performance

What it shows
• Condition monitoring records (vibration, seal flush, pressure, bearing temperature)
• Weekly health status: Normal → Alarm → Trip → Recovery
• KPIs: Availability, MTBF, MTTR, Downtime, PM Compliance, Production Loss

What it can extract
• Degradation trends & time-to-failure signatures
• Reliability metrics per asset
• Alarm/trip breaches and failure onset points

Highlight
Asset degradation caused most critical failures, resulting in downtime, production loss, and financial impact.

How it is useful
• Early failure detection & predictive maintenance
• Asset health scoring & benchmarking
• Connects condition data to downtime and cost impact
Insight
Condition-monitoring data showed early warning signs before failure, enabling predictive maintenance to prevent unplanned shutdowns and improve asset reliability.

<!-- Slide number: 3 -->
Incident Database

What it shows
• Risk incidents with downtime, loss and risk score
• Incident attributes, failure mechanisms, RCA/CAPA status
• Dashboard by plant, discipline and equipment type

What it can extract
• Failure frequency, downtime and loss patterns
• Bad actor assets & recurring failures
• RCA/CAPA progress and risk closure

Highlight
380 incidents caused 2,261 hours downtime and US$67.2M losses, primarily driven by recurring equipment failures such as bearing damage, seal leakage, fouling, and vibration-related issues.

How it is useful
• Prioritize high-risk equipment
• Support reliability analytics and loss reduction
• Link incidents, RCA and corrective actions
Insight
Incident history combined with RCA/CAPA records enables AI to identify recurring failure patterns, prioritize high-risk assets, and recommend preventive actions to reduce future downtime and losses.

<!-- Slide number: 4 -->
Production Data

What it shows
• Hourly PI tag data: rate, pressure, vibration, temperature
• Metadata: units, span, typical value, instrument tag
• Run status and operational behavior

What it can extract
• Operating trends and anomalies
• Equipment condition vs production correlation
• Run/stop behavior and load profiles

Highlight
Production Data captures real-time operational and equipment signals, including feed rate, pressure, vibration, temperature, motor current, and production rate, providing a complete view of plant performance.

How it is useful
• Raw signals for AI & anomaly detection
• Connect performance to output loss
• Foundation for digital twin and monitoring
Insight
Process trends reveal early operational abnormalities before failures or production losses occur, enabling AI-driven anomaly detection, predictive analytics, and faster root cause identification.

RCA & Downtime Data

What it shows
• Structured RCA, CAPA/PAA reports and chronology
• 4P and 4M+1E root cause verification
• Corrective actions and loss closure summary

What it can extract
• Verified root causes & failure mechanisms
• Action plans, PIC and execution status
• Downtime, production and financial impact

Highlight
The database captures verified root causes, failure mechanisms, CAPA actions, responsible owners, and implementation status, creating a valuable repository of reliability knowledge.

How it is useful
• Reliability knowledge hub & lessons learned
• Recurrence prevention and CAPA tracking
• Links failures directly to solutions
Insight
RCA history transforms past failures into reusable operational knowledge, enabling AI-driven similar-incident retrieval, root cause indication, action recommendation, and faster problem resolution.
